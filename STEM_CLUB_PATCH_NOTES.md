# 🚀 Level Up: STEM Club & Makerspace Edition — Patch Notes

**Release Version:** v1.2.0 (STEM Club & Makerspace Update)  
**Target Audience:** Club Sponsors, STEM Teachers, Program Coordinators, and Makerspace Facilitators  
**Applies To:** Elementary (4th–5th Grade) & Middle School STEM / Robotics Clubs  

---

## 📌 Overview
This update tailors the **Level Up: Adventure Mission** platform specifically for hands-on, after-school STEM clubs, robotics teams, and makerspaces. It introduces big-screen projector tools, collaborative squad grading, built-in camera capture for physical builds, collaborative club boss raids, and forgiving attendance-based streaks.

---

## 1. 📺 "Live Club Arena" Big-Screen Projector Mode

### What It Is:
A high-energy, full-screen dashboard designed to be projected onto the club room smartboard, projector, or TV during club meetings.

### How to Access:
1. Log in to the **Teacher Portal**.
2. Select your club class.
3. Click the bright **"📺 Live Club Arena"** button in the top action bar (next to *Levels & Gold Guide*).
4. Click the **Fullscreen icon** in the top-right corner to fill your smartboard display.

### Key Arena Features:
* **⏱️ Station & VEX Pitch Countdown Timer:**
  * Giant high-contrast digital clock (`MM:SS`) visible from anywhere in the room.
  * **1-Click Presets:** `🧹 5m Clean Up`, `⚡ 10m Sprint`, `🏟️ 15m Pitch Pass`, and `🏆 30m Pitch Pass`.
  * **Built-in Arcade Chimes:** Synthesized audio fanfare plays when time expires, signaling students when to rotate off the robotics field or start cleaning up.
* **🎟️ Live STEM Depot & Pitch Queue:**
  * Students spend their earned gold coins in the STEM Supplies Depot to buy field time (e.g., *15-Min VEX Pitch Pass*) or 3D print passes.
  * Pending purchases appear directly on the Arena screen. Teachers can click **"▶ 15m"** to load the pass directly into the countdown timer with the student's name, and click **"Done"** when completed.
* **🏆 Live Guild XP Race:**
  * Animated bar graphs track real-time Guild XP standings across all club teams.
* **👹 Weekly Boss Raid Bar:**
  * Shows the club's progress draining the weekly boss's HP bar together.
* **⚡ Live Activity Ticker:**
  * Recent approved student builds and co-op submissions scroll live across the screen to celebrate achievements.

## 2. 🤝 Squad & Co-Op Submissions (Tagging Teammates)

### What It Is:
In STEM clubs, students frequently build robots, program simulations, or assemble engineering prototypes in pairs or teams. Previously, only the student who submitted the form earned XP. Now, teammates can be tagged and rewarded together.

### How It Works for Students:
1. When submitting an activity, students will see the **"🤝 Built this with a partner or squad?"** box.
2. Clicking **"Tag Teammates"** displays eligible club classmates (guild teammates are highlighted at the top).
3. The student can select up to **4 collaborators** who contributed to the physical build.

### How It Works for Teachers:
1. In the **Submissions Queue**, team builds are badged with `🤝 Co-Op Squad (+X)` and list all tagged teammates.
2. When the teacher clicks **Approve**, the platform automatically:
   * Awards the full activity XP, level-up coins, and guild XP to the leader **and every tagged teammate**.
   * Creates an approved copy of the submission for each teammate so their XP is permanently recorded in their portfolio.
   * Marks the activity as completed on all teammates' choice boards.

## 3. 📸 Built-in "Take Photo" & Auto-Image Compression

### What It Is:
Students can now document physical builds (VEX bots, 3D prints, breadboards, straw towers) directly from their device without dealing with image formatting or upload size errors.

### How It Works for Students:
1. In the **Submit Work** modal, students click the **"Take Photo"** tab.
2. On laptops and Chromebooks with a front or world-facing webcam, students click **"Start Webcam"** and **"Snap Photo!"** to take an instant snapshot.
3. On iPads or mobile devices, students can tap **"Open Device Camera"** to snap directly with their camera app.
4. If a picture doesn't look clear, students can hit **"Retake Photo"** with one click.

### Behind the Scenes for Teachers:
* Chromebook and tablet cameras often produce 5MB–10MB photos that would normally exceed database limits.
* All photos uploaded or captured are **automatically compressed to under 100KB** in real time before sending, eliminating upload failures.

## 4. 👹 Shared Club Weekly Boss Raid HP

### What It Is:
Replaces isolated boss challenges with a collaborative club-wide raid that encourages every student to contribute.

### How It Works:
* Each week, a new Boss Challenge appears with a shared **Club HP Bar** (default `1,000 HP`).
* **Club Damage Mechanics:**
  * Every approved standard mission deals **`50 DMG`** to the Boss.
  * Every approved Boss Challenge submission deals **`250 DMG`** to the Boss.
* Both the student dashboard and the **Live Club Arena** display:
  * Current Boss HP remaining and damage dealt this week.
  * A **Top Raiders** leaderboard displaying the top 3–4 contributors.
  * A **"🎉 BOSS DEFEATED!"** banner once the club reaches zero HP.

## 5. ⚡ Forgiving Session-Based Streaks (8-Day Window)

### What It Is:
Traditional 24-hour daily streaks discourage students in after-school programs that only meet once or twice per week (e.g., every Tuesday or Monday/Thursday).

### How It Works:
* Streaks now operate on an **8-day rolling session window**.
* As long as a student completes an activity on a new calendar day within 8 days of their previous activity, their streak increases (`1 ➔ 2 ➔ 3`).
* Streak badges (`On Fire`, `Unstoppable`, `Streak Legend`) now recognize **3, 5, and 10 club sessions in a row**.

## 6. 🎯 Dynamic STEM Categories in Quests & Badges

### What It Is:
Previously, Daily Quests and path achievements were locked to general academic categories (*Wordsmith*, *Data Scientist*, *Creator*).

### How It Works:
* Daily Quests and achievement badges now dynamically read the **custom STEM categories** you set up in your class (e.g., *Robotics & Automation*, *3D CAD & Printing*, *Coding & Game Dev*, *Electronics & Circuits*).
* Quests will challenge students with categories relevant to your specific STEM curriculum.

## 7. 🛡️ All 15 Guilds Supported on the Leaderboard

### What It Is:
Large STEM clubs and combined grade-level cohorts can now utilize up to 15 distinct guild houses.

### How It Works:
* The student and teacher **Leaderboard** now properly resolves rankings, crests, colors, and member counts for all 15 predefined STEM guilds (`Titanium Tech`, `Quantum Sparks`, `Cyber Wolves`, `Astro Navigators`, `Circuit Phantoms`, etc.).

## 📋 Facilitator Quick-Start Checklist for Your Next Club Meeting

1. **Before Club Starts:**
   * Open the **Teacher Portal** on your smartboard/projector.
   * Click **"📺 Live Club Arena"** and press the **Fullscreen** button.
   * Make sure smartboard sound is unmuted if you want the audio chimes for the timer.
2. **During the Meeting:**
   * Direct students to complete tasks individually or in **Co-Op Squads of 2–4**.
   * If students buy **VEX Pitch** passes or need build timers, click **"15m"** or **"30m"** on the Arena screen queue.
   * Remind students to use the **"Take Photo"** tab to photograph their finished assemblies.
3. **End of Meeting:**
   * Click the **"🧹 5m Clean"** timer button on the Arena screen so students hear the countdown chime for cleanup.
   * Review pending submissions in the Teacher Portal—approving team builds automatically credits the entire squad!

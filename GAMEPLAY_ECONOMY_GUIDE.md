## 2. How Students Earn Experience Points (XP)

| Source | XP Earned | Description / Conditions |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **High Tech Activity** | **150 XP** | Coding, digital design, animation, video recording, or robotics tasks. |
| **Collaboration Activity** | **130 XP** | Pair-programming, group builds, peer critique, or co-authored research. |
| **Self-Reflection Activity** | **120 XP** | Written self-evaluations, learning journals, and goal-setting logs. |
| **Low Tech Activity** | **100 XP** | Hand-drawn diagrams, physical maker builds, flashcards, or posters. |
| **Daily Quest Bonus** | **2x Multiplier** | Matching the rotating daily category doubles activity XP (e.g. up to **300 XP**). |
| **Daily Login / Check-In** | **+50 XP** | Awarded once per calendar day upon completing an activity. |
| **Streak Multiplier** | **+25 XP / day** | Bonus for consecutive active days. Protected by Streak Shields. |
| **Weekly Boss Challenge** | **300 – 350+ XP** | Defeating the class or district collaborative boss challenge. |
| **Mystery Boxes** | **+50 to +200 XP** | Random drops from Mystery Boxes (XP Boost, Surge, or Jackpot). |
| **Achievement Badges** | **+50 to +2,000 XP** | Unlocking milestone badges (Variety Pack, Streak Master, Boss Slayer, etc.). |

## 3. How Students Earn & Spend Gold (Coins)

### Sources of Gold
1. **Level-Up Rewards:** Automatic grant of **25 to 1,000 Coins** whenever a student levels up (7,625 cumulative coins across 25 levels).
2. **Mystery Boxes:** Earned every **3 completed activities** or by defeating a **Boss Challenge**:
   - 🪙 **Coin Pouch:** +25 Coins (Common)
   - 🪙 **Coin Chest:** +50 Coins (Uncommon)
   - 🪙 **Treasure Trove:** +100 Coins (Rare)
3. **Weekly Boss Challenges:** **+100 to +120 Coins** awarded to all participating students upon defeating the weekly boss.
4. **Guild Team Challenges:** **+60 to +100 Coins** per guild member upon completing collaborative guild milestones.
5. **Teacher Grants:** Teachers can directly award custom coin bonuses through the **Avatar & Rewards Inspector** in the Teacher Portal.

### Ways Students Can Spend Gold
1. **Guild STEM Supplies Depot:** Students spend their individual coins to buy physical STEM supplies, robotics modules, and special access passes for their guild:
   - *Tier 1 Supplies:* 40 – 60 Coins (Drafting paper, prototyping cardstock)
   - *Tier 2 Supplies:* 75 – 90 Coins (Structural brackets, fasteners, hardware)
   - *Tier 3 Supplies:* 120 – 150 Coins (DC motors, servo gears, rechargeable power cells)
   - *Tier 4 Supplies:* 180 – 220 Coins (Sensors, microcontrollers, VEX Robotics Pitch Arena access)
   - *Tier 5 Supplies:* 250 – 300 Coins (3D printer filament, custom laser CNC access)
2. **3D Avatar Customization Shop:** Students buy cosmetic hats, outfits, auras, accessories, and special colors to personalize their 3D game character.

## 5. Summary Cheat-Sheet for Teachers

- **Average Activity XP:** 100 to 150 XP
- **Average Time to Reach Level 5:** ~10 to 12 activities (1,400 XP)
- **Coins Earned by Level 5:** 250 Coins (enough for multiple Tier 1-3 STEM supplies or avatar accessories)
- **Coins Earned by Level 10:** 1,175 Coins (enough for high-end Tier 4 & 5 supplies and VEX pitch passes)
- **Audit & Fulfillment:** Every purchase by a student is logged with their name, guild, and timestamp in the **Teacher Portal > Guild Management > STEM Orders** tab, where teachers can mark orders as *Delivered*.
